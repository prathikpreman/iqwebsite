Our Customers

What our customers say about Nibras

Shell Global Solutions Ltd

iNNOVATEQ will start with integrating Shell's Production Universe (PU) software with (Nibras) platform; that integration will enable Shell to sustain PU with cutting-edge technology, eliminate technology dept, optimize its operating costs, and more importantly expanding the realized value of PU within Shell-operated production assets from Deepwater to onshore in 12 different countries across the 4 continents.

Petroleum Development Oman

Nibras has transformed asset management in PDO over the last 6 years generating huge value. This was generated by the unique combined application of Operational Excellence, Lean and Digitalization concepts to the latest technology to achieve significant user and organizational adoption. Nibras today is used by 1,800 users to manage 8,000 wells (12 different types), as well as 75% of PDO's water-flooded reservoirs and 30% of their Surface Facility Equipment. This has released 40% of engineers and operators time, reduced HSE risk by more than 10% and generated at least 5% extra production from PDO.

Renaissance Africa Energy Company Limited

At iNNOVATEQ, we are proud to support Renaissance in Nigeria with the deployment of Production Universe (PU) as part of their digital transformation journey. Our team is delivering a fully integrated solution that empowers Renaissance's operations with real-time production surveillance and data-driven virtual metering. By leveraging PU's scalable, data-driven platform, Renaissance can achieve greater operational efficiency, faster decision-making, and enhanced production performance across their assets.

OQGN

iNNOVATEQ is proud to support OQGN on their digital transformation journey by providing strategic consultancy and roadmapping services. Working closely with their teams, iNNOVATEQ is helping to define and prioritize initiatives that align with their operational goals and long-term vision. iNNOVATEQ's approach focuses on building a clear, phased roadmap that enables OQGN to drive efficiency, enhance data-driven decision-making, and lay the foundation for sustainable digital growth across their operations.
