Our history

Two decades from dashboard to platform company.

Every milestone below happened inside a working oil & gas asset — nothing here was built in a lab.

2002

Shurooq Platform

The first basic web‑based dashboarding system, built to visualize all of PDO's available well surveillance data.

2008

Nibras Platform v1.0

A web‑based data analytics platform delivering Exception Based Surveillance to multiple assets.

2010

Fahud Observer (EBS in Excel)

EBS technology applied to 660 wells in the Fahud Asset — saving roughly one person‑week per month and generating 1% additional production.

2013

Nibras 2.0 — Lean Based Nibras

A Lean‑focused iteration targeting asset owners as customers, with basic EBS and partial Lean workflows. Adoption grew from 65 to 350 users in five months.

2015

Nibras 3.0 — Full Digital Transformation

Automated analytics, prioritized end‑to‑end Lean workflows and management dashboards — serving 1,900 users across PDO's 15 Oil, Gas and EOR assets.

2018

iNNOVATEQ is born

PDO commercializes Nibras through iNNOVATEQ, to share industry best practices and generate in‑country value for Oman.

2019

Global exposure

iNNOVATEQ partners with Shell to launch Production Universe software across 6+ countries and 14 Shell‑affiliated assets.

2020

Nibras NX — fully commercial

A refined, state‑of‑the‑art commercial version built on a decade of digital transformation experience — winning international awards and drawing pilot interest outside Oman.

2021

WRFM Transformation Project — BSP

iNNOVATEQ begins a multiyear contract with BSP for Nibras modules supporting their digital transformation journey.

2025

Continued regional growth

A new office location, a contract signature with Renaissance Africa Energy, and the inauguration of a Qatar branch.
