Reservoir Management

Exception Based Management for Waterflooded Reservoirs

Task forces can fix a reservoir's performance for a quarter. This is what makes the fix last.

The challenge

Water‑flooded reservoir performance analysis took up to two weeks to complete — a serious constraint given that roughly 40% of production comes from waterflood IOR methods. Dedicated task forces could deliver results, but the gains rarely survived their departure.

The solution

Lean workshops established standard reservoir performance KPIs (Res_OE) for waterfloods. "Lean WF Reservoir Management" started in Excel, then migrated to the Nibras platform for sustainability — enabling portfolio‑level monitoring across asset, field and pattern levels, with visual controls highlighting compliance gaps and opportunities.

The impact
  • Portfolio view lets leadership prioritize the highest‑value opportunities
  • Asset teams monitor entire waterflood portfolios at a glance
  • Pilots completed for Gas, GOGD, Thermal and Chemical EOR mechanisms
  • Roadmap in progress toward full Reservoir EBS with end‑to‑end automated workflows
